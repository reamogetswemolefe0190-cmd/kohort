Strict Brand Don'ts (Quality Guardrails)
1. No Floating Card Boxes Never wrap copy in enclosed shaded dashboard boxes with heavy dropshadows (“boxes bafanas”). Content must breathe along open horizontal ledger rules.
2. No Generic Tech Gradients Never use SaaS purple/blue gradients or generic corporate cyan. Palette is strictly Midnight Navy (#101B2D), Warm Paper (#FAF9F5), and Acid Lime (#D3FF65).
3. Never Capitalize Logo Lockup The brand wordmark is strictly lowercase “kohort” with the acid lime square dot. Never write “KOHORT” or “Kohort” inside logo treatments.
