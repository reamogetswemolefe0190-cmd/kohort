# Nova Site: positioning recommendation

Prepared by Kohort · 10 October 2026

**Decision brief:** a recommended starting position, offer copy and next actions. Based on the owner’s intake, saved competitive research and AI-simulated buyer feedback. The recommendation is ready to discuss and apply; customer demand and commercial returns remain unvalidated.

## Our recommendation

**Lead with websites and custom tools for businesses that need a better way to handle bookings and enquiries.** Start with South African service businesses in Pretoria, Johannesburg and Centurion whose owners make the purchase decision and whose work currently depends on WhatsApp, paper or spreadsheets.

This is a recommended focus drawn from the supplied brief and saved positioning analysis, not a finding that this is the largest or most profitable market. Start with a prospect who has a specific operational problem and can describe it. A company’s size or industry alone is not enough to establish fit.

Offer a website and a custom operational tool where both are needed, with separate scopes and charges. Keep a website-only route available. We recommend leading with the problem Nova Site can help address; use the free website promotion as a commercial detail once its scope is understood.

**Why this direction:** the owner reports capability in both websites and custom apps, with direct founder contact. In the latest simulation, the combined message received more selections than either alternative within each scenario. That supports investigating the direction. It does not prove superiority, willingness to pay or a reason to abandon website-only clients.

## The position to communicate

> Nova Site builds websites and custom booking systems or dashboards for South African businesses, with direct contact with the founders who build and support the work.

This proposed positioning uses owner-reported capabilities. Its intended value is tailored work and continuity of contact. Neither is established here as a unique competitor advantage.

Use the buyer’s actual problem to explain the offer. For a business with a booking bottleneck, demonstrate the relevant booking workflow. For a business that only needs an online presence, present the website offer without adding an unnecessary app project.

| Alternative the buyer might consider | The question Nova Site should answer |
|---|---|
| A DIY website route, such as Wix or WordPress | What work will Nova Site take responsibility for, and what will the customer still need to manage? |
| Ready-made booking software | What specific requirement justifies custom development instead of an available product? If there is none, do not assume custom work is preferable. |
| A fixed-price agency or AI-assisted development service | What scope, support and commercial terms can Nova Site demonstrate? The saved research does not establish an advantage against these categories. |
| Keeping WhatsApp, paper or spreadsheets | Is the problem significant enough to justify cost, learning and disruption? Show a relevant example rather than promising savings that have not been measured. |

## Two clear ways to buy

**1. A website for your business**

For a buyer who needs a website without building it themselves. Define the pages, functionality, customer-supplied content, changes and handover before agreeing the work.

The owner’s supplied promotion is a free website build plus free first-year hosting, SSL and maintenance, followed by R1,000 per month for those website services. Promotion eligibility, exact scope, VAT treatment and cancellation terms remain unspecified. Confirm them before using the promotional wording publicly.

**2. A custom tool for a specific workflow**

For a buyer who needs a booking system or dashboard matched to a defined operational problem. Scope that problem, demonstrate relevant work, then provide a separate project quote. Add a website only where needed.

Custom app pricing and ongoing charges are unresolved. The R1,000 website-services fee must not be presented as the price of the custom app or the complete website-plus-app offer. Confirm integrations, migration, support and ownership in the quote rather than implying they are included.

## Copy to use as a starting point

The following copy is newly proposed for testing. It was not the exact wording used in the simulation and makes no promise of measured business results.

**Headline**

> A website for your business. A custom tool for the way you work.

**Supporting copy**

> Nova Site builds websites, booking systems and dashboards for South African businesses. Speak directly with the founders who build and support your project. Start with what you need: a website, a custom business tool, or both. Custom apps are scoped and quoted separately.

**Primary call to action**

> Tell us what you need

**Enquiry prompt**

> Do you need a website, help with a booking or enquiry workflow, or both? Tell us how you handle it today and what you want to improve.

**Website-only alternative**

> Need a website without building it yourself? Talk to Nova Site about a done-for-you website and ongoing support. Request the website scope and pricing.

Use this scope-led copy while promotional terms are being completed. Do not add delivery guarantees, guaranteed savings, “cheapest” claims or a custom-app starting price without supporting evidence and confirmed commercial terms.

## What to do next

| Priority | Nova Site’s action | Completion point |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Complete the two offer sheets: scope, exclusions, fees, VAT treatment, changes, ownership, support and exit terms. Check delivery and support costs. | A prospect can tell what they receive, what they pay and what is quoted separately. |
| 2 | Choose a working example that fits the buyer’s problem and that Nova Site has permission to show. Label demonstration projects accurately. | A short walkthrough shows actual functionality without claiming unmeasured outcomes. |
| 3 | Use the proposed copy in a small set of conversations with relevant prospects. Include website-only prospects separately. | Record the need, alternatives, comprehension, objections and whether a scoped quote is requested. |
| 4 | Review actual quote outcomes and costs before expanding the offer. | Distinguish replies, free-build interest, qualified quote requests and paid commitments. Revise scope or messaging when buyers misunderstand the offer. |

Nova Site’s founders own these actions. No extra swarm run is needed to begin. Customer feedback should guide the next revision, rather than treating a simulated preference as an instruction to change the entire business.

## What this recommendation does and does not establish

The recommended starting point is **a website and a tailored operational tool when the buyer needs both, supported through direct founder contact**. Website-only work remains a valid offer.

The main tradeoff is complexity: a combined offer can address more of a buyer’s needs but also requires a clearer explanation of scope, pricing and responsibility. The recommendation therefore depends on separating website and app costs and showing a relevant example.

Named fixed-price agencies and AI-assisted development alternatives were not adequately covered in the saved research. This brief does not claim Nova Site is cheaper, faster, more capable or uniquely positioned against them. Those gaps limit competitive claims; they do not prevent Nova Site from explaining its own offer clearly.

## Appendix: evidence behind the advice

### Simulation results

The latest run produced 200 AI role-play responses: 50 in each scenario. All compared the same three proposed messages and could keep their existing approach. These are simulated choices, not customer interviews or sales forecasts.

| Buying scenario | Website + custom workflow | Website only | Custom booking only | Keep current approach |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| South African owners: broad website/digital-system needs | 32 | 4 | 0 | 14 |
| South African owners seeking a custom operational app | 25 | 0 | 4 | 21 |
| International business owners | 24 | 4 | 1 | 21 |
| South African office managers / administrative users | 25 | 0 | 0 | 25 |

Scenarios overlap and have no measured population weights; their results must not be combined into market preference percentages. The latest panel had no dedicated website-only scenario, so these counts cannot establish weak demand for website-only services. International buyers and administrative users are separate perspectives from South African purchase decision-makers.

The simulated feedback raised questions about scope, total cost, ownership, ongoing support and the effort involved in moving from an existing system. Selecting the combined offer did not necessarily indicate readiness to switch. These questions informed our recommendation to clarify the offer and show a relevant working example. They illustrate issues to explore with customers, rather than a measured ranking of customer concerns. Some simulated responses also overlooked details already included in the offer, highlighting the need to check these findings with real buyers.

### Source records and confidence

- **Owner-supplied information:** the Nova Site intake describes the services, promotion, target buyers and direct founder support. These capabilities and terms were not independently verified in preparing this brief.
- **Competitive research:** the research considered Wix and WordPress, alongside booking solutions including Allsorts Bookings, Booklink, DoubleDown and Nextapt. Coverage is incomplete and does not constitute an exhaustive competitor ranking. Findings without sufficient supporting evidence were excluded from the recommendations.
- **AI-simulated buyer feedback:** we compared three proposed offers across four buying scenarios, generating 200 simulated responses. All responses used the same AI model, so they may share similar biases. These findings help identify ideas to test with customers; they do not establish real-world demand.
- **Kohort’s interpretation:** the recommended focus, revised copy and next actions are advisory judgments based on those inputs. The revised copy has not been customer-tested. Current competitor prices, willingness to pay, conversion, savings and profitability have not been established.

This positioning brief completes the agreed recommendation and next-step deliverable using the available evidence. A full marketing strategy, media budget and performance forecast are separate work.
