# Nova Site: your positioning feedback

Prepared by Kohort · 14 October 2026

## The recommendation

**Make the customer's problem the starting point: a website, a custom business tool, or both.** Explain these as separate ways to buy, with clear scopes and charges. Keep website-only customers welcome.

Our proposed starting audience is South African service-business owners with a specific booking or enquiry problem, initially around Pretoria, Johannesburg and Centurion. This is a focus to test, not a finding that these are your most profitable customers.

## What to change in your message

Use this as starting copy:

> A website for your business. A custom tool for the way you work.
>
> Nova Site builds websites, booking systems and dashboards for South African businesses. Speak directly with the founders who build and support your project. Start with what you need: a website, a custom business tool, or both. Custom apps are scoped and quoted separately.

**Call to action: “Tell us what you need.”** Ask how the prospect handles bookings or enquiries today, what is causing difficulty, and whether they need a website, an operational tool, or both. This copy reflects the capabilities you supplied and has not yet been tested with customers.

## Make the offer easier to understand

- **Website:** specify pages, features, content responsibilities, revisions, handover and ongoing support.
- **Custom tool:** define one workflow and quote its development and ongoing services separately. Do not imply that custom apps are included in the website fee.
- **Promotion:** before publishing the free-build/first-year offer, complete its eligibility, scope, fees after year one, VAT treatment, ownership and cancellation terms. The supplied R1,000 monthly fee refers to website services, not a complete website-plus-app package.

## Explain why a buyer should choose this route

A prospect can choose DIY website builders, a fixed-price agency, AI-assisted development, ready-made booking software, or their current way of working. Explain the work Nova Site will take responsibility for and show a relevant working example. Recommend custom development only where the buyer's requirements justify it.

Our saved research does not adequately cover named fixed-price agencies or AI-assisted development services. We cannot yet substantiate a claim that Nova Site is cheaper, faster or uniquely better than those alternatives.

## Your next three actions

1. **Complete two offer sheets:** one for websites and one for custom tools. State inclusions, exclusions, fees, responsibilities and support. Confirm that delivery costs make the offer viable.
2. **Prepare one relevant demonstration:** use a real project you have permission to show, or clearly label a demonstration. Show what works without promising unmeasured savings.
3. **Test the explanation with prospects:** record their problem, current alternative, understanding of price and scope, objections and whether they request a scoped quote. Track website-only enquiries separately. Revise the offer using actual quote outcomes and delivery costs.

Nova Site's founders own these actions. For our next discussion, bring the two offer sheets and the example you want to show; tell us where the proposed message does not match what you can deliver.

## What supports this advice

We used your supplied business information, saved competitive research and 200 AI-simulated responses across four scenarios. The combined website-and-tool message received more selections than either other message in each scenario, but many responses retained the current approach. These are synthetic comparisons, not customer interviews, sales forecasts or proof of willingness to pay. There was no dedicated website-only scenario.
