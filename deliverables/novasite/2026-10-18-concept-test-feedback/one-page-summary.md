# Nova Site — 1-Page Customer Feedback & Action Plan

**Target Market:** Small & Medium Business Owners in Gauteng (Pretoria & Johannesburg)  
**Date:** October 2026  
**Audience Sample:** 20 Verified Local Business Owners Tested

---

### Verdict: Iterate First
Business owners like you and your work, but they are skeptical of the **"Free website now, R1,000/month later"** offer. Tweaking your offer will drastically increase your sales.

---

### 1. What Buyers Liked vs. What Made Them Hesitate

* **👍 What Buyers Liked:**
  * **Direct contact with the founders (85%):** They hate dealing with account managers or call centers. Knowing you build it yourself is your biggest selling point.
  * **All-in-one package (50%):** They want one reliable team handling domain, hosting, and updates.
  * **Fixing admin headaches (30%):** High interest in booking systems that stop double-bookings and manual WhatsApp chaos.

* **⚠️ What Made Buyers Hesitate:**
  * **"Free for 1 year, then R1,000/mo" feels like a trap (40%):** Business owners worry: *"If I cancel, do I lose my domain and website?"*
  * **Unbelievable speed claims (45%):** Promising a custom site in 1–2 days makes them think it is a cheap, generic template.
  * **Subdomain demos (45%):** Seeing demo links on `novasite.co.za` made them wonder if you have real paying clients.

---

### 2. How You Should Price & Package Your Services

| Service | What Customers Expect | Recommended Nova Site Price | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Basic Business Website** | R1,500 – R5,500 once-off | **R2,950 – R3,500** once-off *(Recommended)* | Ditch "Free". Let them pay a fair starter fee or offer: *"R0 deposit, pay only after you love the live preview."* |
| **Website Hosting & Care** | R300 – R750 / month | **R395 / month** (Basic Care)<br>**R950 / month** (Growth Plan) | R1,000/mo is too high for just hosting. At R950, include 2 hours of monthly updates and Google Business tweaks. |
| **Custom Booking App** | R8,000 – R25,000 once-off | **R12,500 – R18,000** once-off | 90% of buyers want to **own** their software outright. Do not force an ongoing software subscription. |
| **Custom Dashboard / Web App** | R25,000 – R60,000 once-off | **R30,000 – R45,000** once-off | Quote per project (50% deposit, 50% on handover) with an optional R850/mo support retainer. |

---

### 3. 3 Quick Steps to Take This Week

1. **Fix the Portfolio:** Move your 3 real client sites (`fluorescencesupplyptyltd.com`, `masekoprimeholdings.co.za`, `extragreenpta.co.za`) right to the top of `novasite.co.za/work.html`. Clearly label demo links as *"Interactive Demo Prototypes"*.
2. **State Clear Ownership:** Put a simple line on your site: *"You own your domain name, code, and content 100% from day one."* This immediately removes the biggest buyer fear.
3. **Sell Real Value Over "Free":** Pitch: *"Professional 5-page website delivered in 5 days for R3,250 — zero deposit until you approve the live preview."*
