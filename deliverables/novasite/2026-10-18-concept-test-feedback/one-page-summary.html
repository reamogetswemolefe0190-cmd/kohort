Nova Site — Customer Feedback & Action Plan

Target: Small & Medium Business Owners in Gauteng (Pretoria & Johannesburg)
Date: October 2026
Panel: 20 Business Owners Tested
Verdict: Iterate First
Business owners like you and your work, but they are skeptical of the "Free website now, R1,000/month later" offer. Tweaking the offer will drastically increase your sales.

👍 What Buyers Liked

  • Direct contact with the founders (85%): They hate dealing with account managers or call centers. Knowing you build it yourself is your biggest selling point.
  • All-in-one package (50%): They want one reliable team handling domain, hosting, and updates.
  • Fixing admin headaches (30%): High interest in booking systems that stop double-bookings and manual WhatsApp chaos.

⚠️ What Made Buyers Hesitate

  • "Free for 1 year, then R1,000/mo" feels like a trap (40%): Business owners worry: "If I cancel, do I lose my domain and website?"
  • Unbelievable speed claims (45%): Promising a custom site in 1–2 days makes them think it is a cheap, generic template.
  • Subdomain demos (45%): Seeing demo links on novasite.co.za made them wonder if you have real paying clients.

🚀 3 Steps to Take This Week

  • 1. Fix the Portfolio: Move your 3 real client sites (fluorescencesupplyptyltd.com, etc.) right to the top of novasite.co.za/work.html. Clearly label demo links as "Interactive Demo Prototypes".
  • 2. State Clear Ownership: Put a simple line on your site: "You own your domain name, code, and content 100% from day one." This immediately removes the biggest buyer fear.
  • 3. Sell Real Value Over "Free": Pitch: "Professional 5-page website delivered in 5 days for R3,250 — zero deposit until you approve the live preview."
