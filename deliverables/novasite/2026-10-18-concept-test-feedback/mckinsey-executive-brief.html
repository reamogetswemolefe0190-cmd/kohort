Tier 1: Website Solutions Architecture High Volume

Current Dilemma: "Free Build + R1,000/mo" triggers suspicion of domain hijacking and hidden lock-in.

  • Repackaged Offer: Introduce a transparent R3,250 Launch Starter Package (5 pages, responsive, WhatsApp quote integration, Google Business verification).
  • Risk Reversal Engine: "Zero Deposit Required — Inspect and approve the functional staging preview before issuing final payment."
  • Unbundled Recurring Support:
    • Essential Infrastructure (R395/mo): Dedicated hosting, SSL, daily automated cloud backups, security patches.
    • Partner Growth Plan (R950/mo): Everything in Essential + 2 hours/mo developer changes + monthly local SEO audits.

Tier 2: Custom Web Application Engine High Margin

Current Dilemma: Uncertainty between software subscription rental vs. capital asset expenditure.

  • Definitive Finding: 90% (18/20) of decision-makers categorically reject recurring software rental for bespoke tools.
  • Restructured Commercial Terms:
    • Commercial Booking Systems: R12,500 – R18,000 fixed project fee (50% commencement / 50% live deployment).
    • Enterprise Dashboards & Portals: R30,000 – R45,000 fixed milestone fee.
  • Contractual Guarantee: Explicit IP assignment: "Client assumes 100% legal ownership of custom source code, schemas, and databases upon project handover."
