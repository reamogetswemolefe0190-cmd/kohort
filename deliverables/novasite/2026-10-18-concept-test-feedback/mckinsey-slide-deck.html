[ 1.0 Executive Diagnosis ]

While technical capability is recognized, Nova Site's commercial model triggers defensive buyer skepticism that depresses conversion to 5%

SME decision-makers favor direct founder engineering, but perceive the "free website then R1,000/month" structure as a lock-in trap.

[ Finding 01 ] 85% Resonance
Strong Technical Appeal
  • Direct founder engineering: 85% of evaluated buyers strongly favor working directly with a technical founder over agency account managers.
  • Digital consolidation appetite: 50% of trade and professional practices actively want a single partner to manage web, email, and admin tools.
  • High baseline interest: Concept appeal scored 2.85 / 5.0, proving fundamental demand for modern digital infrastructure.
[ Friction 02 ] 85% Resistance
Acute Commercial Skepticism
  • "Free-to-paid" trap perception: 40% of decision-makers view "free now, pay later" as a hostage setup for domain and hosting.
  • Scope & ownership ambiguity: 60% cited missing terms on who legally owns the source code, database, and .co.za domain.
  • Velocity skepticism: 45% doubted the viability of 1–2 day custom delivery, fearing generic WordPress template reselling.
[ Strategic Pivot 03 ] Immediate Mandate
Commercial Restructuring
  • Drop the "free website" promotion: Replace with an entry capital package at R3,250 (aligned with market equilibrium).
  • Unbundle recurring care: Offer optional, transparent maintenance at R395/month with explicit zero-lock-in portability.
  • Productize custom tools: Anchor high-margin revenue on custom booking dashboards at R15,000 once-off.
Source: Kohort B2B Commercial Evaluation ($N=20$ Stratified Gauteng Decision-Makers; Pretoria & Johannesburg).

[ 5.0 Recommended Product Packaging ]

Restructuring into two distinct commercial streams eliminates lock-in fears and unlocks higher customer lifetime value

Separate transactional brochure sites from high-margin workflow automation systems.

[ Stream A · Cashflow Engine ] Done-in-5-Days
Stream A: High-Velocity Business Websites
  • The Offer: “Done-in-5-Days Business Website”
    • Once-Off Capital Fee: R3,250 (Zero deposit; client approves live functional staging preview before payment).
    • Scope: 5 custom responsive pages, WhatsApp direct lead capture, corporate email setup, Google Business Profile configuration.
    • Ownership Guarantee: Full client legal ownership of domain and assets upon invoice settlement.
  • Unbundled Optional Retainer:
    • Essential Care (R395 / month): High-speed local SSD hosting, SSL certificate, automated daily backups, 30 mins monthly content updates.
    • Growth Care (R950 / month): Essential Care + 2 hours developer enhancements + monthly SEO maintenance.
[ Stream B · Margin Engine ] Workflow Automation
Stream B: Custom Operations & Booking Tools
  • Target Vertical: Operational SMEs losing billable time to WhatsApp booking and manual double-entry (clinics, training academies, trade workshops).
  • Tier 1: Automated Booking System (R12,500 – R18,000 once-off)
    • Direct calendar sync, WhatsApp confirmation pings, online deposits.
    • Measurable ROI: Saves 4–6 administrative hours weekly.
  • Tier 2: Custom Operations Dashboard (R30,000 – R45,000 once-off)
    • Internal workflow dispatch, client portal, asset records.
    • Structure: 40% deposit, 30% testing milestone, 30% production launch.
    • Contract: 100% legal ownership of source code transferred upon final handover.
