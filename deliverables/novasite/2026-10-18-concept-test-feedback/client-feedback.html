2. Direct Answers to Nova Site's 5 Strategic Questions

Q1. Custom Booking Systems & Business Dashboards: Upfront Project Fee vs. Monthly Subscription?

  • Winner: Upfront Fixed Project Fee + Optional Support Retainer (preferred by 18 of 20 decision-makers / 90%).
  • Why: South African SME owners want to own their business system, domain, and data outright and strongly resist open-ended software subscriptions from a young studio (founded Sept 2025).
  • ZAR Price Expectations (from Van Westendorp price-threshold modeling & regional market-anchor telemetry):
  • Defined Booking System (appointments, quotes, WhatsApp/calendar sync): R8,000 – R25,000 once-off fixed fee (split 50% deposit / 50% on live sign-off), plus optional R450 – R850/month hosting & support retainer.
  • Custom Internal / Operations Dashboard (fleet, stock, multi-staff workflows): R25,000 – R60,000 once-off fixed fee, plus optional R800 – R1,500/month SLA retainer.
  • Required Scope & Terms: Written deliverable checklist, 30-day post-launch bug warranty, explicit client ownership of code/database/domain, and 30-day cancellation notice on retainers.

Q2. Website Hosting & Maintenance After Year 1: Is R1,000/month Viable?

  • Verdict: No for basic brochure websites; Yes only if positioned as an active "Growth & Lead-Care Plan".
  • Why: Empirical Gauteng web-services price-anchor telemetry (juicydesigns.co.za, bunnypants.co.za, newperspectivestudio.co.za, procompare.co.za) anchors standard SA SME hosting + maintenance at R299 – R750/month. At R1,000/month (R12,000/year), brochure-site owners indicate they would churn before Month 13.
  • How to Fix the Tiers: 1. Essential Hosting & Care (R395 – R495/month or R4,500/year prepaid): Hosting, SSL, daily backups, uptime monitoring, and 30 mins/month of minor text/photo swaps. 2. Partner Growth Plan (R950 – R1,000/month or R9,900/year prepaid): Everything in Essential + 2 hours/month of development changes, monthly Google Business Profile & local SEO updates, and WhatsApp lead-tracking reporting.

Q3. "Free Website Build" vs. Low-Cost Paid Website Entry Package?

  • Winner: Low-Cost Paid Entry Package (preferred by 17 of 20 decision-makers / 85%).
  • Why: In B2B services, "Free website + free hosting for a year, then R1,000/month" signals hidden lock-in ("who owns my .co.za domain if I leave?") and attracts cash-strapped tyre-kickers who never convert.
  • Recommended Replacement Offer:
  • Launch a transparent R2,950 – R4,950 Once-Off "Done-in-5-Days Business Website Package" (or allow a "Pay R0 Deposit until You Approve the Live Preview" risk-reversal instead of "Free Build").
  • Include full .co.za domain ownership in the client's name, written scope (up to 5 pages + WhatsApp/quote form + Google Business Profile setup), and a transparent R395/month post-launch care plan (first 3 months included).

Q4. Current SME Spend, Approval Authority & Switching Triggers

  • Current Spend: R150 – R1,200/month on domain/hosting/basic tools, and R5,000 – R35,000 every 3–5 years on website rebuilds.
  • Who Approves: The Owner / Managing Director directly (or two co-founders/partners for spend above R15,000).
  • Real Switching Triggers: 1. Losing a lucrative B2B tender or corporate quote because the company lacks a credible .co.za website or professional domain email. 2. A costly double-booking, missed WhatsApp quote, or spreadsheet error during peak season. 3. An existing freelance developer disappearing or taking weeks to make a simple change.

Q5. Best Entry Service to Lead With & Proof Required Before Paying

  • Highest-Margin Entry Wedge: Custom Booking & Quote-Tracking Web Apps for Manual Admin SMEs (clinics, training providers, workshops, catering/events, fleet/trade services) and Custom Workflow Buyers.
  • Highest-Volume Entry Wedge: Transparent Paid Website + Google Business Profile Lead Package for established Gauteng trade/industrial SMEs (fluorescencesupplyptyltd.com, masekoprimeholdings.co.za, extragreenpta.co.za peer profile).
  • Proof Buyers Require Before Paying: 1. Live, clickable client domains at the top of novasite.co.za/work.html (move subdomain concept demos into a separate "Interactive System Prototypes" section so buyers don't mistake demos for fake clients). 2. 1-page written scope & ownership guarantee (confirming the client owns their .co.za domain and data from day one). 3. 2–3 short before/after case studies showing real operational outcomes (e.g., "Cut weekly WhatsApp booking admin by 4 hours" or "Generated first 10 qualified quote requests").
