### 3.2 Key Quantitative Metrics
* **Overall Appeal:** **2.85 / 5.0** (Strong appreciation for founder contact and all-in-one web support).
* **Believability:** **2.40 / 5.0** (High skepticism around "1–2 day custom delivery" and subdomain demo links).
* **Purchase Intent:** **2.20 / 5.0** (Only 5% top-2 intent; 85% bottom-2 reluctance toward Year-2 R1,000/mo lock-in).
* **Optimal Price Point (OPP - Van Westendorp):** **R3,021.75** once-off for standard business websites.
* **Acceptable Website Pricing Range:** **R1,500 – R5,500** once-off.
* **Custom Web Application Price Expectation:** **R8,000 – R25,000** once-off for booking tools; **R25,000 – R60,000** for internal multi-user dashboards.

### 3.3 Core Strategic Takeaways & Diagnosed Friction
1. **The "Free Year-1" Offer Backfires in B2B:** 40% of business decision-makers view "Free website now, R1,000/month after year 1" as a deceptive lock-in trap, questioning domain ownership and code transfer rights.
2. **Subscription Resistance on Custom Apps:** 90% (18/20) of SME owners insist on owning custom booking and operational tools outright via a fixed build fee rather than paying perpetual SaaS rent.
3. **Price Anchoring Gap on Hosting:** Gauteng market benchmarks anchor brochure site maintenance at R299–R750/month; R1,000/month is rejected unless it explicitly bundles active growth services (monthly SEO & development hours).
