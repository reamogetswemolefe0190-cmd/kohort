# Kohort
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
Existing Python CLI and standalone HTML/Markdown reports. The report explorer extends this output format with local browser interactions.

## Users
Agency operators prepare recommendations for business owners. Clients need a clear position, usable messaging and next actions with inspectable evidence.

## Product Purpose
Turn a business brief, competitive research and synthetic buyer comparisons into useful positioning advice across industries.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Research and swarm commands already exist. Client brief synthesis is currently editorial. Synthetic responses are not customer interviews, market shares or sales forecasts. Preserve separate scenario results, source limitations and release status. Use saved runs for this prototype; do not trigger new paid research.

## Brand Commitments
Kohort. Extend the existing neutral report styling and violet accent. The user approved studying MiroFish's report experience and building a Kohort version with executive recommendations, navigable sections, side-by-side options and expandable evidence.

## Evidence on Hand
Saved advisory and swarm JSON, including swarm-e566f9e0; client positioning brief in deliverables/novasite/2026-10-10-positioning-brief. Live customer validation and a full marketing strategy are outside this prototype.
