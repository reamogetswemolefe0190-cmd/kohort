# Nova Site intake notes

The accompanying `novasite.yaml` matches Kohort's current Brief schema. Detailed
evidence and constraints are retained in its description because the schema has
no dedicated evidence or resource fields. The owner's email is omitted from the
model input because it is unnecessary for the business decision. Supporting
links are preserved as supplied; their contents were not inspected during intake.

## Clarifications before interpreting pricing results

- What revenue has been collected, from how many clients, and for which services?
- What are the cash cost and founder hours for a typical website and each web app?
- What exactly does the R1,000/month plan include: changes, support hours,
  monitoring, backups, response times and hosting limits?
- What qualifies a client for the partner rate and two included change hours?
- What are the offer's scope, cancellation, ownership and VAT terms?
- Are the 30 available hours per week shared between both founders?
- What cash budget and deadline are available for the next customer test?
- Which stage option did the owner select? The pasted form lists all options
  without marking a selection. Delivered work is reported; paid sales are unclear.

## Interpretation limits

Website acquisition, website recurring support and custom web app pricing are
different decisions. The intake preserves them but does not supply a single
headline price. For a focused price experiment, create a separate brief for one
clearly scoped package and its proposed price. Combining these services into one
willingness-to-pay estimate would make the result difficult to interpret.

Client reviews, agreements, project counts and enquiry counts are owner-reported.
They support further investigation but do not establish paid demand or renewal.
Synthetic interviews can generate hypotheses and objections; real buyer offers,
payments and renewal behaviour are needed to validate prices and conversion.
